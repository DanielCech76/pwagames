# Long-distance AR catalog

Source: https://www.geonames.org/ — CC BY 4.0.
Official source ZIP URLs and hashes are in index.json.
Global cities: population at least 100,000 or national capitals.
Mountains: explicit elevations at least 1,500 m in the listed nine countries, spatially thinned to the highest available point in a 20 km neighbourhood. No prominence values were inferred. This is not complete worldwide mountain coverage.
Extended row columns after the seven v1 fields: population or null, national-capital flag, long-distance-major flag.
